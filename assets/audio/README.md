# Sound effects

Selected, trimmed and peak-normalized to -3 dBFS, mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV.
Original filenames and event mapping are in sources.json.

All selected effects are CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- Free Firearm Sound Library: Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney.
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  pistol.wav: 1911/A_34P.wav, first shot. shotgun.wav: Mossberg/N_26P.wav, first shot.
- Shotgun Reload Sound Effects: zer0_sol.
  https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
- 150 Combat Sound Effects: curated by untamedpixels; original creators and licenses in COMBAT-LICENSES.txt.
  https://opengameart.org/content/150-combat-sound-effects

Music is separate from these sound effects and is not covered by their CC0 license.

The shotgun is a derived layered effect: Mossberg shot pitched down 10%, bass EQ/compression, a short low-frequency explosion body and a quiet delayed rack. Peak -1.5 dBFS; layer sources are bossdeath and reload in sources.json.
